LocalFlow — unsigned pilot, macOS 14+, US English

1. Extract this ZIP. Run: bash ./install.sh
2. Open ~/Applications/LocalFlow.app in Finder. Approve through macOS.
3. Complete the four permission buttons in LocalFlow Setup.
4. If local speech assets are missing, provision English (US) through macOS
   Keyboard > Dictation setup, then verify LocalFlow while disconnected.
5. Focus a text field. Hold Control–Option–Space, speak, release all keys.

Escape cancels. Never submits. No clipboard use. Ten minutes maximum.
The destination app can store or transmit the inserted text.

The installer is offline and requires no compiler, package manager, or sudo.
It does not strip quarantine or disable Gatekeeper. Unsigned updates can
require fresh macOS permission grants. Quit LocalFlow before an update.

Rollback: bash ./install.sh --rollback
Uninstall: bash ./uninstall.sh
Previous apps remain in ~/Applications/LocalFlow Backups.
